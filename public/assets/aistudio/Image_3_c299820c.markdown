## Brand & Style

This design system serves a modern Indian travel and mobility platform specializing in curated tours, bespoke holiday packages, and self-drive or chauffeured SUV/car rentals across India. 

### Brand Personality & Emotional Intent
- **Adventurous yet Reassuring:** Evokes the thrill of navigating high-altitude passes in Ladakh or coastal stretches in Goa, balanced with operational reliability and punctuality.
- **Warm & Hospitable:** Reflects contemporary Indian hospitality—generous, welcoming, and deeply attentive to comfort.
- **Trustworthy & Clear:** Eliminates booking anxiety through straightforward pricing in INR (₹), transparent specifications, and rapid communication channels.

### Design Movement
The interface blends **Modern Minimalism** with **Warm Tactile Nuances**:
- Crisp, intentional layout architecture with expansive whitespace to allow high-vibrancy travel photography to breathe.
- Pill-shaped call-to-actions, softened card contours, and low-contrast surface boundaries.
- Ambient, diffused lighting models paired with clean line iconography to convey safety, polish, and seamless digital utility.

## Colors

The palette draws from the natural contrasts of the subcontinent: deep Himalayan pinewoods and coastal waters grounded against warm desert sunsets.

### Primary Palette (Deep Teal)
- **Primary Base (`#0F766E`):** Represents balance, security, and prestige. Applied to headers, key navigation anchors, active state highlights, and primary brand signifiers.
- **Primary Dark (`#115E59`):** Reserved for hover states on primary actions, pressed states, and high-emphasis focal text over light containers.
- **Primary Light Container (`#CCFBF1`):** Subdued surface fill for travel tags, selected search filter pills, and subtle badge backdrops.

### Secondary Palette (Sunset Orange)
- **Secondary Base (`#F97316`):** The primary conversion color. Applied strictly to direct action items such as "Book Now", "Check Availability", and price callouts.
- **Secondary Dark (`#EA580C`):** Used for hover and pressed states on secondary conversion buttons.
- **Secondary Soft (`#FFEDD5`):** Used as surface backing for promotional deals, discount callouts, and limited-time offer badges.

### Tertiary & Status Accents
- **WhatsApp Accent (`#25D366`):** Dedicated exclusively to immediate support interactions, direct booking queries, and the persistent floating concierge module.
- **Success (`#16A34A`):** Instant confirmation states, booking verified status, and vehicle sanitization assurances.
- **Warning (`#F59E0B`):** Limited seat/vehicle counters and seasonal weather/road advisories.
- **Error (`#DC2626`):** Form validation alerts, payment cancellations, and unavailable date indicators.

### Neutrals & Surfaces
- **Canvas / Background (`#F8FAFC`):** Crisp off-white tone mitigating glare across bright displays.
- **Surface / Card Background (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white container surfaces providing lift from canvas.
- **Text Heavy (`#0F172A`):** Deep slate providing high readability for headlines, trip summaries, and rental terms.
- **Text Muted (`#64748B`):** Supporting metadata, fuel policies, luggage limits, and secondary timestamps.
- **Border Slate (`#E2E8F0`):** Fine structural definition across table headers, input boundaries, and card perimeters.

## Typography

The type system pairs a geometric, high-clarity headline font with a neutral, utilitarian workhorse for content and data-dense itinerary layouts.

### Font Hierarchy
- **Headlines & Display:** Set in a rounded, geometric display face to establish welcoming, contemporary personality. Large headings maintain tight negative letter spacing to preserve compactness and optical balance against imagery.
- **Body & Numerical Data:** Rendered in an unadorned grotesque family with high x-height, guaranteeing legibility across vehicle specification grids, policy footnotes, and currency notations (₹).

### Currency & Numerical Handling
- All currency figures use medium or semibold weights with standard letter spacing to prevent crowding between the Rupee glyph (`₹`) and numerical figures (e.g., `₹4,499 / day`).
- Destination highlights, day-wise itineraries, and duration tags rely on standard body weights with uppercase or title-case micro-labels for scanning efficiency.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model leverages a dynamic 12-column grid system bounded within a max-width container of `1280px` to maintain optimal viewing angles on wider monitors while keeping focal points disciplined.

### Breakpoints & Layout Adaptations
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid. Hero sections pair multi-tab booking search bars overlaid directly on scenic photography. Sidebars handle active booking summaries and vehicle filters (Transmission, Fuel, Seating capacity). Gutters fixed at `1.5rem` (`24px`), outer canvas margin at `2rem` (`32px`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout. Search modules stack horizontally into a clean 2x2 cluster. Itinerary listings reflow into dual-column cards. Gutters transition to `1rem` (`16px`).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with an edge margin of `1rem` (`16px`). The central booking widget transforms into a structured step-by-step card. Critical callouts (like day rates and "Book Now") dock to a sticky bottom sheet.

### Spacing Philosophy
Consistent vertical progression ensures predictable grouping:
- Content directly tied to a label uses `space-xs` (4px).
- Icon-and-text associations use `space-sm` (8px).
- Internal component card padding relies on `space-md` (16px) or `space-lg` (24px).
- Section partitions, carousel separations, and content module boundaries use `space-xl` (40px) or higher.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses softly diffused ambient shadows and crisp perimeter definitions, avoiding harsh artificial elevations.

### Surface Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** Used by default page canvas (`#F8FAFC`). No elevation or drop shadows.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** Pure white surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) sitting on the canvas. Outlined with a fine `1px` border of Border Slate (`#E2E8F0`) paired with an extra-soft ambient drop shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05);`
- **Level 2 (Interactive Overlays & Booking Engine):** Applied to the hero booking widget, active dropdown menus, and popovers:
  - `box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04);`
- **Level 3 (Floating Elements & Concierge Actions):** Reserved for the floating WhatsApp button and sticky mobile checkout bars:
  - `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 10px 10px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04);`

### Border Accents
Borders function as subtle edge crispeners rather than heavy containers. Cards always combine a thin boundary line (`#E2E8F0`) with a low-opacity shadow to preserve distinct shape boundaries against both bright photography and clean canvas surfaces.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates a dual-tier curve treatment: structural content containers use standard soft roundedness, while direct interaction elements embrace full pill geometry.

### Radius Definitions
- **Structural Cards & Content Containers:** 14px to 16px corner radius (`rounded-lg` scale). This creates an approachable, modern travel frame that softly cushions photography and multi-row itinerary lists.
- **Buttons, Filter Chips, and Badges:** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px` radius). Emphasizes tap targets, reinforces friendliness, and distinctly separates dynamic interactive targets from background cards.
- **Form Input Fields:** Standardized to 10px–12px radius, balancing the pill-shaped CTA above or beside them while maintaining clean baseline alignment across booking tables.

## Components

### 1. Booking Search Widget
- **Tabs:** Dual-tab header switching between "Vehicle Rental" and "Tour Packages". Inactive tab sits flat on slate background; active tab renders with pure white fill, semibold text in Deep Teal, and an indicator line or soft shadow.
- **Fields:** Split grid inputs covering Pickup City, Dates/Durations, and Vehicle Class (e.g., Hatchback, Sedan, 4x4 SUV). Focus state introduces a 2px stroke in Deep Teal with a faint teal halo ring.
- **CTA:** Full-width or inline pill button in Sunset Orange (`#F97316`) reading "Search Cars" or "Explore Tours".

### 2. Buttons
- **Primary Action (Conversion):** Pill shape. Background Sunset Orange (`#F97316`), text pure white, font-weight 600. On hover: shifts to Dark Orange (`#EA580C`). Focus ring: 2px offset with 2px Sunset Orange.
- **Secondary Action (Navigation / Details):** Pill shape. Deep Teal (`#0F766E`) background with white text, or pure white background with a 1.5px Deep Teal border and Deep Teal text.
- **Floating WhatsApp Concierge:** Fixed pill/circular button positioned at `bottom: 24px; right: 24px; z-index: 50`. Background WhatsApp Green (`#25D366`), white icon and text label ("Chat with Expert"). Elevated with Level 3 shadow.

### 3. Cards (Tours & Vehicles)
- **Vehicle Rental Card:** 
  - Top half: 16:9 aspect vehicle image showcasing realistic clean models (e.g., Scorpio-N, Thar 4x4, Innova Hycross).
  - Badges positioned over top corners: Fuel type, Transmission, and "Zero Security Deposit" tag.
  - Middle: Vehicle model headline, seat count, luggage capacity, and feature icons.
  - Bottom strip: Daily starting rate in INR bolded (`₹3,200 / day`), accompanied by a pill CTA "Book Now".
- **Tour Package Card:** 
  - Destination hero image (e.g., Jaipur Fort, Ladakh Pass, Alleppey Backwaters).
  - Floating duration tag (e.g., "5 Nights / 6 Days") on a frosted or solid white pill tag.
  - Title, star rating with review counts, trip inclusion icons (Hotel, Sightseeing, Transfers).
  - Total package cost highlighted in Sunset Orange.

### 4. Chips & Filter Tags
- Rendered in full pill radius.
- **Default State:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px `#E2E8F0`, text `#0F172A`.
- **Selected State:** Background Light Teal (`#CCFBF1`), border 1px `#0F766E`, text `#0F766E`, semibold.

### 5. Input Fields & Selectors
- Height standard: 48px to accommodate comfortable mobile thumb tap targets.
- Interior padding: 16px horizontal.
- Accompanied by Lucide line icons anchored on the left (e.g., Calendar, MapPin, Clock, Users) rendered in `#64748B`.

### 6. Badges & Status Indicators
- **Instant Confirmation:** Pill container, `#DCFCE7` background, `#16A34A` text.
- **Fast-Filling / Limited Slots:** Pill container, `#FEF3C7` background, `#B45309` text.
- **All-India Permit:** Pill container, `#CCFBF1` background, `#0F766E` text.
